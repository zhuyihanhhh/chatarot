## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Built-in spreads
The system SHALL offer 17 spreads defined in `data/spreads.json` (the 16 spreads of taluo.net plus the holy triangle), grouped into categories: daily (daily card, yes/no, three cards), general (horseshoe, Celtic Cross, Star of David), love (relationship, love, love pyramid), decision (holy triangle, career, decision, two choices), cycle (four seasons, year ahead), and spirit (chakra, seven planets). Each spread SHALL define positions with name, meaning and layout coordinates, a `readingOrder`, and a `guide` describing its interpretation method.

#### Scenario: Filter by category
- **WHEN** user clicks a category tab on the home page
- **THEN** only spreads of that category are shown

#### Scenario: Year-ahead months
- **WHEN** the year-ahead spread is laid out
- **THEN** position labels show concrete months starting from the reading month

### Requirement: Reading and flip order
Card positions SHALL be determined by draw order. Flip order SHALL NOT change any card meaning. The table SHALL highlight the next suggested card following the spread's `readingOrder`, "全部翻开" SHALL reveal in `readingOrder`, and the user's manual flip order SHALL be recorded and sent to the AI as a hint about the user's focus.

#### Scenario: Suggested next card
- **WHEN** the Star of David table is shown
- **THEN** position 1 is highlighted, and after it is revealed position 3 is highlighted

#### Scenario: Manual flip order in prompt
- **WHEN** user flips position 1 manually, then reveals the rest with "全部翻开", and requests a reading
- **THEN** the prompt contains the suggested reading order, the spread guide, the manual flip order "第1位", and the rule that flip order does not change meanings

#### Scenario: Choose spread
- **WHEN** user opens the app
- **THEN** the four spreads are listed with name, card count and description, and user can select one

#### Scenario: Celtic Cross crossing card
- **WHEN** Celtic Cross is laid out
- **THEN** position 2 is rendered rotated 90° across position 1

### Requirement: Question entry
The system SHALL let the user enter a question before shuffling; the question MAY be empty, in which case a general reading is requested.

#### Scenario: Empty question
- **WHEN** user proceeds without a question
- **THEN** the reading context marks the question as "综合运势/无特定问题"

### Requirement: Cryptographic shuffle and orientation
The system SHALL shuffle the 78 cards with Fisher–Yates driven by `crypto.getRandomValues`, and assign each card an independent orientation with 50% reversed probability unless reversals are disabled in settings. Card results MUST NOT be generated by the LLM.

#### Scenario: Reversals disabled
- **WHEN** user disables reversals in settings
- **THEN** all drawn cards are upright

#### Scenario: No duplicates
- **WHEN** n cards are drawn
- **THEN** all n stems are distinct

### Requirement: Draw ritual
The system SHALL present shuffle animation, a cut step (choose one of three piles), a face-down fan of the remaining deck from which the user picks n cards, placement in position order, and per-card flip.

#### Scenario: Pick cards
- **WHEN** user clicks a face-down card in the fan
- **THEN** that card moves to the next empty position, and picking stops when all positions are filled

#### Scenario: Flip card
- **WHEN** user clicks a placed face-down card
- **THEN** it flips with animation and its Live2D instance mounts in the drawn orientation

#### Scenario: Reveal all
- **WHEN** user clicks "全部翻开"
- **THEN** remaining cards flip in position order

### Requirement: Card detail
The system SHALL show, for a revealed card, its name, orientation, position meaning and keywords.

#### Scenario: Inspect card
- **WHEN** user hovers or taps a revealed card
- **THEN** a detail panel shows name, 正位/逆位, position name and orientation keywords
